Referências: wordmark e ícone Pilo. Marca própria dotfiles com d aberto, ponto central e i azul. PNG sobre fundo claro sólido; não é vetor nem possui fundo transparente.

```text

This is a NEW clean master, not an edit of previous heavy rounded drafts. Make it MUCH lighter and more elegant: medium/regular rounded geometric monoline lettering, substantial open counters, fluid curves, air between all letters. Think quiet confidence of a premium contemporary personal software brand, not inflated bubble letters, not a sportswear logo.

Output: one wide horizontal logo lockup centered on a perfectly flat solid off-white #fcfcfc canvas, approximately 2048x640. Modest balanced margins. No transparency, mockup, texture, grain, gradient, lighting, dimensional effects, multiple alternatives or presentation annotations. All contours clean and precisely antialiased.

Left: an ORIGINAL compact electric-blue #339cff symbol with a round geometric dot integrated into a small asymmetric open "d" / hidden-file glyph. Construct it from a near-circular rounded open bowl and a short upright stroke with a small purposeful break that exposes the interior dot. It must read as a new dotfiles identity, not the reference p-shaped icon turned upside down, not an f, command prompt, bracket, cloud, shield, folder or infinity symbol. Strong simple silhouette, 2–3 components maximum, legible small.

Right: the exact lowercase word "dotfiles" in near-black #181818, exceptionally balanced monoline rounded geometric typography with open e and smooth single-storey forms where appropriate. Weight close to the Pilo reference wordmark (regular/medium), not bold or heavy. The i-dot is electric-blue and acts as a subtle connection to the standalone symbol. Clear space between symbol and wordmark. No extra punctuation after the name, no slogan, no other text.

The symbol and wordmark are one coherent identity. Desired result: beautiful, precise, memorable, friendly and restrained, with the polish of a professional independent design studio. Use the Codex palette only: #181818, #fcfcfc, #339cff.
```
